# Codex image brief: AOHYS interior-page lens textures

Use your native image generation tool (`image_generation`). Write outputs only
into `.impeccable/mocks/lens-textures-20260925/`. Do not write code, do not
edit other files, do not commit.

## What these images are for

The AOHYS public site (Horizonte pastel direction) shows one refractive glass
sphere on each interior page. The sphere is rendered with WebGL: it magnifies
and bends a square image, breathes slowly and follows the pointer. Each image
below is the texture seen through that sphere. Only the central ~65% circle is
visible, so the subject must sit in the middle with calm, softly lit edges.

Attached references: `horizonte.png` (the approved home comp: bright white
page, one translucent sphere, daylight) and `ref-user-pin.png` (the owner's
mood: Scandinavian daylight, white walls, light wood, small mustard accents).

## Shared style (all five)

- Square 1024x1024. Photographic still life with a soft, slightly painterly
  finish; bright, airy, clean; natural morning daylight from the upper left;
  gentle leaf shadows allowed.
- Palette: white and warm café/brown (#473C33 as the darkest tone, used
  sparingly) with pastel honey #FCE3A6, pastel olive #D6E2B4 and pastel apricot
  #FDD2B1. Light oak wood and white linen are welcome. No blue, cyan, violet,
  neon, black backgrounds, heavy gradients or glassmorphism.
- One protagonist per image, centered, generous calm space around it.
- No people, no hands, no faces. No readable text, letters, numbers, logos or
  UI screenshots anywhere.
- Consistent family: same light, same lens, same materials across the set.

## Deliverables

1. `lens-resume.png` — Career. Top-down view of a light oak desk: an open
   notebook with fine pencil wireframe sketches (boxes and lines only, no
   words), three small pastel sticky notes (honey, olive, apricot), a slim
   pencil and a white ceramic cup of coffee. Feeling: years of building, calm
   and organized.
2. `lens-practice.png` — Practice. A small elegant system of pastel ceramic and
   light-wood modular blocks (cubes, slabs, one cylinder) assembled into a
   balanced composition on white linen, like a product being built from parts.
3. `lens-contact.png` — Contact. Two white ceramic coffee cups facing each other
   on a white table by a sunny window, a small closed notebook and pen between
   them, a sprig of olive leaves. Feeling: an inviting conversation.
4. `lens-privacy.png` — Privacy. A pane of frosted glass in soft daylight with a
   sealed pastel-apricot envelope resting behind it, only its silhouette
   readable through the frost; a small brass key on white linen in front.
   Feeling: clear boundaries, calm trust.
5. `lens-architecture.png` — Architecture / Development System. A precise
   physical architectural model: five thin translucent acrylic plates tinted
   honey, olive and apricot, stacked with even gaps and joined by fine brass
   pins, casting soft colored shadows on white. Feeling: layered system,
   engineered with care.
